Seu guia na ilha

Franco rema e faz caminhadas por Ilha Grande há anos. Cada passeio é realizado com um guia e no máximo seis pessoas, para que ninguém fique para trás e a ilha permaneça tranquila.

Não é necessária nenhuma experiência para nenhum dos passeios. Você receberá uma aula de remada antes de sair, todos os equipamentos de segurança e as fotos e vídeos do passeio enviados para você depois.

Swim through the stars

Tiny marine organisms light up when the water moves. We paddle out after dark, slip into the sea with a mask, and the water lights up around your hands with every stroke.

Bioluminescence is a natural phenomenon, so it depends on the sea and the sky. Darker nights show more light, which is why departure times move with the phase of the moon.

The rest of Karamuru

Same canoe, same small group, different light. Both trips leave from Vila do Abraão.

Sunrise paddle

We leave before dawn and paddle to a spot to watch the sun come up. Snorkel over the coral on the way, then follow the coastline home past birds, beaches and forest.

  • Departs 5:00 AM, shifts with the season
  • Group 6 people max
  • Price R$150 per person

An easy evening on the water along the coast of Ilha Grande, timed so you are out there when the light turns. A good first paddle if you have never been in a canoe.

Pico do Papagaio

A guided climb through the Atlantic Forest to one of the best views in the state of Rio de Janeiro. We warm up at the meeting point, then walk up through the forest at a pace that works for the group.

On the way there is wildlife to spot, plants you will not see anywhere else, and lookouts over the whole bay. At the top we stop properly before heading back down.

  • Local guide the whole way
  • Photos and videos included
  • No climbing experience needed
